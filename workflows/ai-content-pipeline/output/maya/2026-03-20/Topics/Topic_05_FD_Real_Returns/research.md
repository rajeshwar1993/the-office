# Research: Your FD Is Losing You Money — The Math Your Bank Won't Show You

## Topic Overview
Fixed deposits are India's most popular savings instrument, but post-tax and post-inflation returns are often negative. This topic walks through the actual math and presents alternatives.

## Key Data Points

### FD Rates (Current, March 2026)
- Major banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI) offering 6.5-7.25% on 1-3 year FDs
- Senior citizen premium: +0.5% typically
- Small finance banks: up to 8-8.5% but with higher counterparty risk

### Tax Impact on FD Returns
- Interest fully taxable as "Income from Other Sources"
- TDS at 10% if interest > 40,000/year (50,000 for senior citizens)
- Effective post-tax return for 30% bracket: ~4.9% on a 7% FD
- Effective post-tax return for 20% bracket: ~5.6% on a 7% FD

### Inflation Context
- CPI inflation: ~6.5% (elevated due to global oil crisis, supply chain pressures)
- Food inflation running higher at ~7-8%
- RBI target band: 2-6% — currently above upper band
- Oil price impact: crude above $100/barrel driving transport and input costs

### Real Return Calculation
- 7% FD → 4.9% after tax (30% bracket) → -1.6% real return (6.5% inflation)
- Even at 20% tax bracket: 5.6% - 6.5% = -0.9% real return
- Historical context: In the 1990s/2000s, FD rates of 10-12% with 4-5% inflation gave genuine positive real returns

### Alternatives Discussed
1. **Debt Mutual Funds**: Post-indexation tax treatment after 3 years, no annual TDS, typically 7-9% pre-tax returns
2. **Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs)**: 2.5% annual interest + gold price appreciation, zero LTCG at maturity, government-backed
3. **Equity Index Fund SIPs**: Nifty 50 historical CAGR of 12-14% over 5+ year periods, volatility smoothed by SIP averaging

### Banking Profit Model
- Banks lend FD deposits as personal loans (12-16%), home loans (8.5-9.5%), credit cards (24-42% APR)
- Net interest margin for Indian banks: 3-4%
- Relationship managers receive sales incentives for FD mobilization

## Trend Relevance (March 2026)
- Oil crisis driving inflation above RBI comfort zone
- RBI has not raised repo rate proportionally — FD rates lag inflation
- Real returns gap widening — making this an urgently relevant topic
- Retail FD deposits continue growing despite negative real returns — awareness gap is real
