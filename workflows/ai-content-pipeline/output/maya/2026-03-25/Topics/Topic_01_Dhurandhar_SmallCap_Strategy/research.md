## Angle & Genre Rationale
**Angle:** The Dhurandhar 2 cultural moment creates a perfect "aggressive vs timid" framing for financial behavior. The "Chhuchhundar" metaphor (timid, playing it safe) contrasts with "Dhurandhar" (bold, strategic aggression). This allows comedy_satire to carry real financial math without feeling like a lecture.

**Genre (comedy_satire):** The Bollywood tie-in gives permission to be playful, use callbacks ("Chhuchhundar" becomes a running joke), and deploy misdirection (setting up the "safe" narrative before demolishing it with math). The Bihar Boy Math framing makes the numbers feel like a storytelling device rather than a spreadsheet.
