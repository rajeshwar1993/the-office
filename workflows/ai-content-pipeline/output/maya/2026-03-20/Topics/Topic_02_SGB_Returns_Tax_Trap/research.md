# Research: SGB Investors Made 312% Returns — But There's a Tax Trap Coming

## Key Facts & Data Points

### SGB 2019-20 Series IV Performance
- Purchase price: 3,840 per gram
- RBI premature redemption price: 15,814 per gram (March 2026)
- Total return: approximately 312% over ~6.5 years
- Plus 2.5% annual interest on face value paid semi-annually
- Total effective return including interest: approximately 330%+

### How SGBs Work
- Sovereign Gold Bonds issued by RBI on behalf of Government of India
- Denominated in grams of gold, minimum 1 gram
- 8-year maturity with exit option after 5th year
- 2.5% annual interest paid semi-annually
- Redeemable at prevailing gold price set by RBI
- Can be traded on stock exchanges (secondary market)

### The Tax Trap — Budget 2026 Changes
- Previously: SGBs held to maturity = completely tax-free capital gains
- Previously: SGBs bought on secondary market and held to maturity = also tax-free
- Budget 2026 change effective April 1: Tax exemption removed for secondary market buyers
- Primary subscribers (bought directly from RBI) holding to maturity: still tax-free
- Secondary market buyers now taxed as per applicable capital gains rules
- This is a significant change for traders and secondary market investors

### Who Wins
- Original subscribers (primary market) holding to maturity — no change, still tax-free
- Those who bought early series at low gold prices — massive returns, tax-free if held to maturity
- SGB 2019-20 Series IV holders redeeming now — 312% return, completely tax-free

### Who Loses
- Secondary market buyers who purchased SGBs on NSE/BSE
- People who bought SGBs at a premium on exchanges hoping for tax-free treatment
- Future secondary market buyers — must now factor in capital gains tax
- This reduces the attractiveness of SGBs as a trading instrument

### Gold Price Context (March 2026)
- Gold prices experiencing volatility amid Middle East tensions
- March 2026 seeing a price correction — "crash" narrative in media
- Question: Is this a buy-the-dip moment or a value trap?
- SGBs are no longer being issued in new tranches (RBI paused issuance)
- Only secondary market available for new SGB buyers

### Tax Calculation Impact
- On 1 lakh investment becoming 4.12 lakh:
  - Primary holder at maturity: Zero tax, full 3.12 lakh gain
  - Secondary buyer post April 1: Capital gains tax on 3.12 lakh
  - At 12.5% LTCG (if held >12 months): approximately 39,000 tax
  - At 20% (if classified differently): approximately 62,400 tax
- The tax treatment makes a material difference in net returns

### RBI's SGB Position
- RBI has been reluctant to issue new tranches
- Gold price rally means redemptions are expensive for the government
- Some speculation that SGB program may not see new issuances
- Existing SGBs on secondary market trading at various premiums/discounts
