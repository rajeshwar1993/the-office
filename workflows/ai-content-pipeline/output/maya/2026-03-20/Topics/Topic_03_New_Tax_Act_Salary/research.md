# Research: 8 Tax Changes Hitting Your Salary from April 1

## Topic Overview
The Income Tax Act 2025, passed by Parliament in March 2025, replaces the Income Tax Act 1961 — the first complete overhaul in 64 years. Effective from April 1, 2026 (Tax Year 2026-27).

## Key Facts & Data Points

### Structural Changes
- Old Act: 800+ sections, 14 schedules
- New Act: 536 sections, 16 schedules
- "Assessment Year" replaced with "Tax Year"
- Tax Year = the year income is earned (April to March)

### HRA Metro City Expansion
- Previously metro (50% HRA): Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata
- Newly added metros: Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad + 4 others
- Non-metro HRA exemption remains at 40%

### Commute Benefit
- Employer-provided transport/travel allowance for commuting now tax-free
- Applies to cab services, metro reimbursements, travel allowances

### Default Tax Regime
- New tax regime is now the default
- Employees must actively opt for old regime
- Old regime: higher rates, more deductions (HRA, 80C, home loan)
- New regime: lower rates, minimal deductions

### Section 87A Rebate
- Taxable income under 12 lakh: zero tax under new regime
- Rebate applies automatically — no separate claim needed
- Standard deduction of 75,000 remains unchanged

### Leave Encashment
- Exemption limit for non-government employees: 25 lakh
- Previously a government notification; now codified in the Act

### Penalty Framework
- Consolidated scattered penalty provisions
- Clearer consequences for late filing and incorrect claims
